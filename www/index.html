Backups

  
A backup is taken automatically once a day, after 1:00 am, the first time Kharcha is open. The last 7 are kept inside the app, and each nightly backup is also saved as a file in Documents/Kharcha on your phone, so it survives even if the app is cleared. Restoring first saves your current entries, so you can undo it.
